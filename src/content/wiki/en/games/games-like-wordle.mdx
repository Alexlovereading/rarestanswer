These puzzles borrow a morning habit more than a grid. One puzzle, the same one for everybody, short enough to finish with coffee, and a result you can share without spoiling the answer. Wordle is the five-letter word game now living on The New York Times site. The games below keep that ritual and then go somewhere else. Facts about Wordle itself are summarized from its Wikipedia article and the New York Times Games help page, checked on 14 November 2026.

## Which games kept the once-a-day habit?

Wordle gives you six attempts to guess a five-letter word. After each guess, tiles change colour. Green means that letter is in the word and in the right spot. Yellow means the letter is in the word in a different spot. Grey, which the help page spells Gray, means it is not in the word. Extra copies of a letter are marked green or yellow only when the answer contains them that many times. Guesses have to be real words. There is a hard mode that forces you to reuse any green or yellow letters you have already found. The daily word is the same worldwide, and the help page says it releases at midnight in your time zone. A high-contrast mode swaps the usual colours for players who need it. The help page says daily words are curated by editors using the Oxford Dictionary, and that you play it on the web at nytimes.com/games/wordle, in the Games app, or in the News app.

Josh Wardle, a Welsh software engineer, released it publicly in October 2021. The New York Times Company acquired it on 31 January 2022, for a price reported as a low seven-figure sum, and kept the daily puzzle free. Tracy Bennett became its editor in November 2022. Wikipedia gives a curated answer list of 2,309 words, and says seven words were later removed from an original list of 2,315, with a shift toward American spelling. The copies that followed are why this search is crowded. This page stays with games whose rules I could verify.

## Which verified games share the daily habit?

[Rarest Answer](/) is the one on this site, and it is honest to put it in the list as a different task. You do not hunt a hidden string. You name something that fits a prompt, and a rarer accepted answer scores more. The daily round is seven prompts, shared, resetting at local midnight. Tiers run from Common at 5 points to Legendary at 100. There is a free [unlimited mode](/unlimited/), which Wordle does not have. When you want to study what scored, open [rarest answers](/rarest-answers/). Play it if the fun you wanted from Wordle was the shared day, and the fun you wanted from the answer was a fact rather than a spelling.

[Krillion](/games/krillion/) is the daily dive at krillion.io. Seven rounds, open answers, a top gem the FAQ calls the Krillion. [Cosmillion](/games/cosmillion/) is seven prompts, 25 seconds each, and its homepage presents the game to people looking for games like Wordle. Its top rank is One in a Galaxy. [Seldom](/games/seldom-game/) is one question and five guesses, and the better score is the lower one. Its about page names Wordle directly as the kind of daily game that only rewards the single correct result.

[Dartle](/games/dartle-game/) is one category and a sum of exactly 100, with up to three answers. [Farflung](/games/farflung-game/) is five map pins, and a less obvious country travels further. The [Size It Up game](/games/size-it-up-game/) is five visual scale guesses and no timer. The [Majority game](/games/majority-game/) asks you to type what most people typed. [Wonderhop](/games/wonderhop/) is four of these ideas in one day: common, rare, oldest, newest. [Jinxed](/games/jinxed-game/) is the multiplayer version of the rare-answer prompt. [Ball Knowledge](/games/ball-knowledge-game/) includes a football surname puzzle it calls Football Wordle: six tries, one puzzle a day, on ball-knowledge.com. That is a themed word grid sitting inside a larger quiz site.

[Brainer](/games/brainer/) is Gamebunny's daily trivia show. Its public pages confirm the daily game and do not confirm Wordle-style letter tiles, so it belongs in the "daily" column only.

## What did the clone wave actually add?

Wikipedia's account of early 2022 names a few variants with a paper trail. Absurdle, by the programmer qntm, can still move the target while staying consistent with earlier feedback. Quordle uses several boards at once, and Merriam-Webster later acquired it. Worldle asks for a place. Heardle asks for a song, and Spotify acquired it. The Times has treated the Wordle name as its mark. A random app-store page with Wordle in the title is not the puzzle at nytimes.com/games/wordle. For a rare answer rather than a word, stay on this site.

## Is there an unlimited mode?

Official Wordle is one new puzzle a day. The help page says you do not need a subscription for that daily puzzle. It also says the rest of the Wordle experience — the archive, Wordle Bot, and creating a puzzle — is for Games, All Access, and Home Delivery subscribers. The archive goes back to 19 June 2021. Playing an archived puzzle changes your played count, win rate, and guess distribution, and it does not change your streak. That is a library of past words, not an endless button that mints a new one. An account is how stats follow you across devices.

For another round the same day, switch games or open [unlimited mode](/unlimited/). Extra [Rarest Answer](/) prompts train the "what else fits?" step, which [how to find rare answers](/guides/how-to-find-rare-answers/) covers. A rare-answer comparison is on [games like Krillion](/games/games-like-krillion/).

On the grid itself, spend early guesses on new letters and keep any green or yellow letter if you turned hard mode on. Wikipedia, citing New York Times data, lists adieu, audio, stare, raise, and arise among common openers, and notes that slate, crane, and trace tended to finish in fewer guesses than adieu or audio. The day's word is still one word. Read the colours.
