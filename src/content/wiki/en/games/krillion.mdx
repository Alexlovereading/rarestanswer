The Krillion game is a daily trivia dive in the browser at [krillion.io](https://krillion.io/). A less familiar valid answer is meant to outscore the obvious one. This unofficial guide was checked against that site on 14 November 2026.

## What is Krillion?

The homepage calls it "the daily dive." The privacy policy, effective 23 September 2026, says you can play without an account, a name, or an email. A random id in the browser tells players apart. A finished dive stores the game, the score, and that id, which powers the "better than X% of divers" line. Guest answers on the daily are checked and then discarded. Answers on the free practice dive are kept with that id so scoring can be tuned.

A Discord recap in the same policy can include a seven-round dive graph. That is the clearest official statement of the length: seven rounds. The FAQ does not publish a per-round clock for the daily dive. The timer it does explain belongs to Puffer Party. Fan sites quote a 25-second daily clock and a six-tier point table. Those figures are not on the FAQ, the privacy policy, or the Puffer Party page, so they are left out here.

The FAQ says one person makes the game, posting as kp, and describes himself as a 23-year-old American computer-science graduate. The same answer says other people have claimed to be the creator, and that search has listed someone who made a copy. Business mail is business@krillion.io and support is support@krillion.io. Merch is at shop.krillion.io. The menu links [Chainle](https://chainle.io/), described there as a word-association game by the creator's younger brother.

## How do you play the Krillion game?

Open [krillion.io](https://krillion.io/) and start the daily dive. The FAQ says an app is planned, not shipped. Guest progress, sound, a colorblind mode, your character, and purchase unlock tokens live in the browser. Clearing site data clears them. A Google account is optional and syncs completed games to other devices.

Type an answer that fits. The server checks it. If a fair answer is missing, the FAQ says a source was missed. Autocorrect is generous because some prompts have thousands of answers, many with aliases. The published example is Threebanded Sheephead, aliased as "sea donkey," one letter from "sea monkey," so a near-miss can snap to a creature you did not mean.

The Discord Activity is a separate save. It uses your Discord user id and display name for the dive, answers, score, history, and streak. Channel standings and recaps are optional. A recap can show a name, a score, and the seven-round graph. It does not include the answers.

## How does a rare answer get its score?

Answer sets are aggregated from dictionaries, wiki pages, encyclopedias, and whatever else the question needs, then merged and cleaned. Some prompts are scored against a familiarity database of how often a word or name shows up in what people read and write. Others get language-model help plus a manual pass.

The Krillion is the gem, and it is not chosen on rarity alone. Past a certain depth, the FAQ says, rarity gets hard to separate, and some collected answers are so obscure that almost nobody would type them. The gem is also picked because it is satisfying to land, and because at least a few players might actually enter it. That is why the Krillion is sometimes less rare than some deep cuts. "Too clever" is the go-to obscure pick that is not actually obscure.

The packs page showed At the Movies and Music when this guide was checked. Any unlock, for any pack, turns ads off across the site. Ads are handled by Mediavine. More packs are planned. The FAQ says the current focus is answer-set curation and rarity selection.

## Is there an unlimited mode?

There is a path at [krillion.io/unlimited/classic](https://krillion.io/unlimited/classic). The page still titles itself as the daily dive, and it did not expose a written rules sheet. The privacy policy separately describes a free practice dive. This guide does not claim those are the same mode, and it does not state a price.

Extra rounds with rules published on this site are [unlimited mode](/unlimited/) for [Rarest Answer](/): seven shared prompts, tiers from Common through Legendary, and lists under [rarest answers](/rarest-answers/). A wider map is on [games like Krillion](/games/games-like-krillion/).

## What is Puffer Party?

[Puffer Party](https://krillion.io/party) is labeled "Krillion's multiplayer category game." You name an answer and pass the puffer before it pops, with friends or in a public room. The last player still floating wins.

The hidden fuse is random on purpose, between 10 and 30 seconds under the default settings, and the timer is shared. Pass with 20 seconds left and the next person inherits them. If fewer than 8 seconds remain, it goes back to 8. After a pop, a new fuse starts. The maker cites hot potato and JKLM's BombParty. Lag can make the puffer look alive after the turn has already moved. The FAQ still says a fuller Krillion multiplayer, and a party game based on Krillion, are planned. Puffer Party is the multiplayer category game already public.

## What do Krillon, Noillirk, and "one in a krillion" mean?

The site spells the name Krillion. Krillon, and Krillian, are spellings people type for that same game. A lookalike domain is a different product.

Noillirk is the name reversed. [playdaily.org/noillirk](https://playdaily.org/noillirk) is a survey game that says the common answer scores. Apple's App Store lists an iPhone app, "Noillirk - Reverse Krillion," from BlueMind Software. Neither is krillion.io.

Players often say "one in a krillion" for the top of the board. The FAQ's words are "the Krillion" and "the gem." The [One in a Million game](/games/one-in-a-million-game/) search did not find a current daily game under that title.
