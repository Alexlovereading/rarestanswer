Krillion is a daily trivia dive in the browser at [krillion.io](https://krillion.io/). A less familiar valid answer is meant to outscore the obvious one. This unofficial guide was checked against that site on 16 November 2026. Other dailies that are not this game are on [games like Krillion](/games/games-like-krillion/).

## What is Krillion?

The homepage title that day was "Krillion · the daily dive." The daily screen was labeled Dive #124. The menu lists Daily dive, Unlimited, Puffer Party, Archive, Themed packs, My Krillion, Friends, and FAQ. There is no separate Help item; the FAQ is the help page. The homepage meta description reads: "Seven prompts a day, same for everyone. Rare answers sink you deeper. Obvious ones barely count," and then the tagline in this page's title.

The [privacy policy](https://krillion.io/privacy), effective 23 September 2026, says you can play without an account, a name, or an email. A random id in the browser tells players apart. A finished dive stores the game, the score, and that id, which powers the "better than X% of divers" line. Guest answers on the daily are checked and then discarded. The policy says the game is not directed at children under 13. A Discord recap can include a seven-round dive graph. That graph is the policy's picture of a finished dive.

The FAQ does not publish a per-round clock. The homepage how-to list does, and it is quoted in the next section. The timer the FAQ explains belongs to Puffer Party. The FAQ still does not publish a six-tier point table.

The FAQ says one person makes the game, posting as kp, and describes himself as a 23-year-old American computer-science graduate. The same answer says other people have claimed to be the creator, and that search has listed someone who made a copy. Business mail is business@krillion.io and support is support@krillion.io. The menu links a merch store, and it links [Chainle](https://chainle.io/) as a word-association game by the creator's younger brother. An app is planned, not shipped.

## How to play Krillion?

Open [krillion.io](https://krillion.io/) and start the daily dive. The how-to list on that homepage, opened on 16 November 2026, says:

- 7 prompts a day. Same for everyone.
- 25 seconds to name one thing.
- Rare answers score big. Obvious ones float.
- The pick that feels clever is one the school thought of too.
- Every point sinks you 10 metres. 700 is the trench.
- One dive per day. No second chances.

The line under the title says the same length in shorter form: "7 prompts · 25 seconds each · rarer answers sink deeper."

Type an answer that fits. The privacy policy says every answer you type is sent to the server to be checked. If a fair answer is missing, the FAQ says a source was missed. If it is not accepted, it did not score. Autocorrect is generous because some prompts have thousands of answers, many with aliases. The published example is Threebanded Sheephead, aliased as "sea donkey," one letter from "sea monkey," so a near-miss can snap to a creature you did not mean. Look at the answer that was accepted before you treat that snap as the gem you intended.

Guest progress, sound, a colorblind mode, your character, and purchase unlock tokens live in the browser. Clearing site data clears them. A Google account is optional. The privacy policy says it can sync completed games to other devices.

The Discord Activity is a separate save. It uses your Discord user id and display name for the dive, answers, score, history, and streak. Channel standings and recaps are optional. A recap can show a name, a score, and the seven-round graph. It does not include the answers.

Rarity is not the same measurement. The FAQ on krillion.io says it depends on the question. Some prompts are scored against a familiarity database of how often a word or name turns up in what people read and write. Others get a combination of language-model help and a manual pass. The top gem is not chosen on rarity alone. [Rarest Answer](/) ranks answers inside one prompt by Wikipedia pageviews and stores a tier. The round scores that stored tier. It does not use that familiarity database, and it does not choose the top answer for being satisfying. A score on krillion.io is not a score on this site. ## What do Krillion's rules say about a rare answer?

Answer sets are aggregated from dictionaries, wiki pages, encyclopedias, and whatever else the question needs, then merged and cleaned. Some prompts are scored against a familiarity database of how often a word or name shows up in what people read and write. Others get language-model help plus a manual pass.

The gem is not chosen on rarity alone. Past a certain depth, the FAQ says, rarity gets hard to separate, and some collected answers are so obscure that almost nobody would type them. The gem is also picked because it is satisfying to land, and because at least a few players might actually enter it. That is why the gem is sometimes less rare than some deep cuts. "Too clever" is the go-to obscure pick that is not actually obscure. If the rare name you reach for is always the same catchphrase, other players are reaching for it too.

The how-to line that 700 is the trench does not list six point values. The FAQ does not publish the 5-to-100 table used on this site. Do not treat that trench line as this site's tiers, or the reverse.

## How does the game on this site differ?

[Rarest Answer](/) is a different product. This site is not affiliated with Krillion. A score on one is not a score on the other.

Krillion sends each answer you type to its server to be checked. This site checks guesses in the browser against published prompt data. Streak and round stats stay in localStorage on the device. They are not uploaded to a Rarest Answer server. Optional Google sign-in on Krillion can sync completed games. This site does not offer an account. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) says this game is not directed at children under 13 either. Optional ads, analytics, and comments load only when their environment values are set, and they stay outside the play card.

A daily round here deals up to seven prompts, the same set for everyone on your local date, and fewer than seven when the catalog cannot fill seven. Six tiers run from Common at 5 points to Legendary at 100. One Spotlight boost doubles a single scored prompt. The [rules](/guides/how-to-play-rarest-answer/) are the long version, including the point table. The builder ranks answers inside one prompt by Wikimedia pageviews and stores a tier. The running game scores that stored tier. It does not add a fun factor on top. The great-ape list's source is Wikidata plus Wikipedia pageviews (2026-10). The names and stored tiers are on [great ape species](/answers/great-ape-species/). A method for producing a less famous legal name on this site is the [finding guide](/guides/how-to-find-rare-answers/).

Krillion's FAQ does not publish the 5-to-100 table. A gem there can be less rare than a deeper cut on purpose. Their homepage how-to calls 700 the trench without listing these six tiers. A Legendary row here is the top stored bucket on that prompt, not a promise that it is the most endangered, the most clever, or the most fun. Neither description is a license to paste the other game's answer key.

Play Krillion if you want their dive, their gem, their packs, their archive, or Puffer Party, and if you are willing to send each guess to their server. Play here if you want the published point table, a round that stays on this device, and [unlimited](/unlimited/) or category practice that does not touch the daily streak. ## Is Krillion free?

The daily dive is described as playable with no account. The privacy policy's name for one stored practice round is a free practice dive: answers are kept with the random id so scoring can be tuned. That is the closest wording to a free Krillion session on the pages checked. It is not a statement that every screen is free, and the policy does not say that practice dive is the Unlimited screen.

The [packs page](https://krillion.io/packs) is headed "Themed packs · beyond the daily dive." It says "Free for now · BETA" and "Answers & scoring may have issues." The sets on the page were Words & Language (English), 30 chapters; At the Movies, 30 pictures; Sports, 30 games; and Geography, 30 expeditions, with the line "around the world in seven answers." Music was marked coming soon. The FAQ says more packs are planned, and that the current focus is answer-set curation and rarity selection. The same FAQ says any unlock, for any pack, turns ads off across the site. The privacy policy says advertising is handled by Mediavine, and that card details go to Stripe, not to the game's servers. A beta pack is a poor place to memorize a gem, because the page says scoring may be wrong.

The archive URL is the screen that shows a price. On 16 November 2026 [krillion.io/archive](https://krillion.io/archive) did not list old dives. It showed "Unlock Unlimited · $5" and "one time. any unlock removes ads across the whole site." The packs page still describes that archive as "Every aired daily, resurfaceable." This guide did not buy the unlock, so it does not describe a past dive. A remembered gem would not be tomorrow's prompt anyway.

On 17 November 2026 that screen titled itself "The Endless Dive," with the line "dive after dive · rarer answers sink deeper." It was labeled Endless Dive #1. Krillion endless is that screen, not a second product.

The word infinite is not on that screen. An infinite Krillion mode, and a Krillion infinite setting, could not be confirmed as names the site uses. The menu only places an infinity symbol beside Unlimited.

[Unlimited mode](/unlimited/) on this site deals a new set without waiting for local midnight, uses the same tiers and the same 25-second clock, and does not move the daily streak. You can turn the timer off. You can also play one category. Finished prompts are listed under [rarest answers](/rarest-answers/). That mode is not theirs.

## Does Krillion have 1v1 or multiplayer modes?

[Puffer Party](https://krillion.io/party) is on the site now. On 16 November 2026 its title was "Puffer Party · Multiplayer Category Game · Krillion," and the page said: "Krillion's multiplayer category game. Name an answer and pass the puffer before it pops. Play with friends or join a public room. Last one floating wins." The homepage badge on that link read "BETA · LIMITED TIME."

The party's own how-to says to answer before the puffer explodes, that rarer answers get you closer to an extra heart, and that you cannot reuse an answer for the current question. The FAQ says the hidden fuse is random, between 10 and 30 seconds under the default settings, and that the timer is shared. Pass with time left and the next person inherits it. If fewer than 8 seconds remain, it goes back to 8. After a pop, a new fuse starts. The maker cites hot potato and JKLM's BombParty. Lag can make the puffer look alive after the turn has already moved. That room rewards a fast legal name, which is often the common one. It is a poor place to practice the gem.

The same page has a size control. Choosing 1V1 changes the label to "one on one." That control is Krillion 1v1 on Puffer Party. It is not a separate rules page. The other sizes read "three at a table," "4 to 6 players," and "8 to 10 players." Rated is labeled "near your rating · it moves." Quick Play is "unrated · just for fun." You can host a public listed room or join with a code, and a public room list was on the page.

A Krillion battle royale, a Krillion battle mode, and PvP Krillion could not be confirmed on the homepage, the FAQ, or Puffer Party. A mode named group Krillion could not be confirmed either. The [Friends page](https://krillion.io/friends) asks you to sign in with Google "to add friends and compare daily scores and answers," and it says "Your leaderboard is just you and your friends." That is a scoreboard, not a match.

The FAQ still answers "Are you going to make Krillion multiplayer?" with "Eventually, yes," and says a party game based on Krillion is in the planning stages. That answer and the live Puffer Party page were both on the site the same day. This guide does not guess which description will still be true later.

This site has no room and no pass-the-object timer. After a round here you can copy a share grid of colored squares and a score. The grid does not include the answers.
