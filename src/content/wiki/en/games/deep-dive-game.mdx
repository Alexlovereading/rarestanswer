A Deep Dive game search points at more than one product. One is a browser trivia page. One is an arcade diver that has been on portal sites for years. Several better-known daily games only borrow the words. This page separates them using pages I could open on 14 November 2026. If a guide mixes their scores, it is mixing different games.

## What is the Deep Dive game?

The trivia page is [Deep Dive Trivia](https://www.frisson-labs.com/static/games/deep-dive-trivia/index.html), titled on the page as Deep Dive Trivia · Otomates. I could not find a studio biography, a release date, or an app-store listing to put next to that title. What follows is what the page itself states.

You have 25 seconds to name one thing that fits each prompt. There are seven prompts. The on-page example is a colour in the rainbow: Red earns 10 points, Indigo earns 100, and both are correct. The less obvious choice is meant to take you farther. The printed tiers are Common 10, Uncommon 15, Rare 30, Very rare 60, Epic 85, and Legendary 100. Each point is 10 metres down. Seven perfect answers reach a 7,000-metre trench. The page says points use fixed rarity tiers so everyone has the same scoring, and that answer percentages show real players' first responses, including passes and timeouts, with small samples labeled.

Aliases and unambiguous small typos are accepted. The daily resets at 4 a.m. California time. Once a prompt opens, its clock keeps running even if you leave. Between prompts you can take your time. Past dives keep their original questions and leaderboards. Your first completed attempt is saved. Replays are unranked. The page says past dives do not award daily Bits. A score graph compares completed attempts on the same daily dive.

Those tier names overlap words this site also uses. They are not this site's scores. [Rarest Answer](/) uses Common 5, Familiar 10, Uncommon 20, Rare 40, Epic 70, and Legendary 100. A screenshot that says Legendary 100 might be either game. Check the domain.

The arcade game is the other Deep Dive that portal sites agree on. Listings on Y8 and POG describe a browser game in which you dive, collect coins and air bubbles, watch an oxygen meter, and avoid sea creatures. POG credits the developer as Zygomatic, lists it as an HTML5 arcade game, and shows an "added" date of 19 March 2021. Other portals give different dates, so this page does not treat any of them as a studio release announcement. There is no trivia prompt and no rare-answer score. The listing does not publish a control scheme, so this page does not invent one. It is a run you can start again. It is not a daily shared puzzle.

## Is Krillion a Deep Dive game?

[Krillion](/games/krillion/) calls itself the daily dive. That subtitle is why the searches collide. The game lives at krillion.io. Its FAQ talks about "deep cuts" as answers past the obvious ones, and about a top gem called the Krillion. Playing a page called Deep Dive Trivia will not submit a score to Krillion, and a Krillion dive will not move the submarine on the Otomates page.

[Ball Knowledge](/games/ball-knowledge-game/) has a mode called Deep Cuts. It is a football question, five shots, and a rarer player scores more. The words are close. The sport is the clue that you are on a different site.

Some unofficial Krillion-style sites also use "deep dive" in a tier list. Krillion's own FAQ warns that other people have claimed to be the creator. This page does not copy those tier lists.

## Is there an unlimited mode?

On the Deep Dive Trivia page, the way to play again is a replay or a past dive. The page says the first completed attempt is the saved one and that replays are unranked. That is a limited kind of extra play, and it is not described as a generator of brand-new prompts. The arcade game can be restarted because it is a single run with no daily lock.

If you want another full rare-answer round after the daily set, use [unlimited mode](/unlimited/) here. [Rarest Answer](/) deals seven-prompt rounds beyond the one shared day. [Cosmillion](/games/cosmillion/) sells unlimited launches as part of an optional All Access purchase. [Jinxed](/games/jinxed-game/) says you can keep playing free after its three-prompt Daily Brew.

## Which one should you open?

Open krillion.io if someone sent you "the daily dive" and meant Krillion. Open the Otomates page if you want the seven-prompt game that prints the 10-to-100 table and the 4 a.m. California reset, and treat it as that page, not as a famous studio release. Open a portal's Zygomatic game if you want the oxygen-and-coins diver.

For a rare-answer round with answer pages you can read afterward, play [today's round](/) and then the [rarest answers](/rarest-answers/) list. The method is the same on any of the trivia versions: a real answer, one step past the obvious, before the clock dies. [How to find rare answers](/guides/how-to-find-rare-answers/) stays on that step. [Games like Krillion](/games/games-like-krillion/) is the index of the games that published a real home page. [Seldom](/games/seldom-game/) and [Wonderhop](/games/wonderhop/) are the other verified ways to chase a rare name, with their own scoring direction and their own clocks.
