Krillion and the game on this site look alike for about one sentence. You name something that fits, and a rarer legal answer is supposed to beat the obvious one. After that sentence the products split: who makes them, where the answer is checked, what a top score means, and what "unlimited" actually documents.

This page is the split, checked on 14 November 2026. It is not an official Krillion guide. Their site is [krillion.io](https://krillion.io/). Ours starts on the [homepage](/). A longer unofficial read of their FAQ is the [Krillion page](/games/krillion/). Other dailies that are not either game are on [games like Krillion](/games/games-like-krillion/).

## What does each site say it is?

Krillion's homepage menu lists Daily dive, Unlimited, Puffer Party, Archive, and Themed packs. The visible line under the menu is "one in a krillion." The packs page titles the product "the daily dive" and its description says: "Seven prompts a day, same for everyone. Rare answers sink you deeper. Obvious ones barely count."

This site is a free browser trivia game. A daily round deals up to seven prompts, the same set for everyone on your local date, and fewer than seven when the catalog cannot fill seven. Six tiers run from Common at 5 points to Legendary at 100. One Spotlight boost doubles a single scored prompt. There is no account. The [rules](/guides/how-to-play-rarest-answer/) are the long version. The [scoring page](/guides/how-scoring-works-in-rarest-answer-games/) is the arithmetic. This site is not affiliated with Krillion. The privacy policy says that in those words.

## Who makes Krillion, and where do answers go?

The [FAQ](https://krillion.io/faq) says the game is one person, posting as kp, a pseudonym, who describes himself as a 23-year-old American computer-science graduate. The same answer says other people have claimed to be the creator. Business mail is business@krillion.io and support is support@krillion.io. An app is planned, not described as shipped. Merch is linked from the menu.

The [privacy policy](https://krillion.io/privacy), effective 23 September 2026, says every answer you type is sent to Krillion's server to be checked. Guest answers on the daily are checked and then discarded. Answers on the free practice dive are kept with a random browser id so scoring can be tuned. A finished dive stores the game, the score, and that id, which powers a "better than X% of divers" line. Optional Google sign-in can sync completed games. Advertising is handled by Mediavine. Any pack unlock turns ads off across the site. The policy says Krillion is not directed at children under 13.

This site checks answers in the browser against published prompt data. Streak and round stats stay in localStorage on the device. They are not uploaded to a Rarest Answer server. The [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) says the game is not directed at children under 13 either. Optional ads, analytics, and comments load only when their environment values are set, and they stay outside the play card. Those are different data stories. Do not assume a habit that is safe on one site is the same habit on the other.

## How is a rare answer chosen?

Krillion's FAQ says answer sets are aggregated from dictionaries, wiki pages, encyclopedias, and whatever else the question needs, then merged and cleaned. Some prompts are scored against a familiarity database of how often a word or name turns up in what people read and write. Others get language-model help plus a manual pass. The top pick is called the Krillion, or the gem. Past a certain depth, the FAQ says rarity gets hard to separate, so the gem is also picked because it is satisfying to land and because a few players might actually type it. The phrase "too clever" is defined there as the go-to obscure pick that is not actually obscure. Autocorrect can snap a near miss onto an alias you did not mean. The published example is Threebanded Sheephead, aliased as "sea donkey," one letter from "sea monkey."

This site's builder ranks answers inside one prompt by Wikimedia pageviews and cuts them into the six tiers above. The running game scores the tier stored on the answer. It does not send the guess to a server, and it does not apply a fun factor on top of the rank. The great-ape list's source is Wikidata plus Wikipedia pageviews (2026-10). The names and stored tiers are on [great ape species](/answers/great-ape-species/). A method for producing the less famous legal name is the [finding guide](/guides/how-to-find-rare-answers/).

Neither description is a license to paste the other game's answer key. A gem on Krillion can be less rare than a deeper cut on purpose. A Legendary row here is the top stored bucket on that prompt, not a promise that it is the most endangered, the most clever, or the most fun.

## What does unlimited, archive, and party mean on each side?

Krillion's menu has an Unlimited path. The URL [krillion.io/unlimited/classic](https://krillion.io/unlimited/classic) opened on 14 November 2026 and showed the same daily-dive shell. It did not expose a written rules sheet. The privacy policy separately describes a free practice dive. This page does not claim those are the same mode, and it does not state a price. The packs page says themed packs are "Free for now · BETA," warns that answers and scoring may have issues, and lists four open packs: Words and Language (30 chapters), Movies (30 pictures), Sports (30 games), and Geography (30 expeditions). Music is marked coming soon. Archive is described there as "Every aired daily, resurfaceable."

[Unlimited mode](/unlimited/) on this site deals a new set without waiting for local midnight, uses the same tiers and the same 25-second clock, and does not move the daily streak. You can turn the timer off. You can also play one category. That is a rules sheet, because it is this game.

Puffer Party, at [krillion.io/party](https://krillion.io/party), is "Krillion's multiplayer category game." You name an answer and pass the puffer before it pops. The FAQ says the hidden fuse is random, between 10 and 30 seconds under the default settings, and that the timer is shared. A fuller Krillion multiplayer, and a party game based on Krillion, are described as planned, not as the thing already public. This site has no room, no pass-the-object timer, and no friends list. After a round you can copy a share grid of colored squares and a score. The grid does not include the answers. Ideas that use that grid, labeled as house rules, are on [party ideas](/guides/rarest-answer-party-ideas/). The Roblox experience with a similar slogan is a third product, covered on its [own page](/guides/rarest-answer-party-roblox/).

## Which differences should decide where you play?

Play Krillion if you want their dive, their gem, their packs, their archive, or Puffer Party, and if you are willing to send each guess to their server. Play here if you want the published 5-to-100 table, a round that stays on this device, and unlimited or category practice that does not touch the daily streak. Playing both is reasonable. Treating a tactic that worked on one as a documented rule of the other is how the comparison goes bad.

The [one in a krillion strategy](/guides/one-in-a-krillion-strategy/) page stays inside what Krillion's FAQ actually says. It does not smuggle this site's point values back onto their gem.
