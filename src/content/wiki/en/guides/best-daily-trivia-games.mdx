These puzzles are not one genre, and mixing them up is how a recommendation page goes bad. Some hide one word and tell you how close you are. Some hide a place. Some hand you a category and pay more when the correct answer is the less-viewed member of a stored list. On this site that rank is Wikipedia or Wiktionary pageviews, not a count of what other players typed. This roundup keeps those apart. Every game below returned a page on 15 November 2026. The one-line description is taken from that page's own title or description.

The rarest-answer daily on this site starts on the [homepage](/). Rules are on [how to play](/guides/how-to-play-rarest-answer/). If you want more Wordle-style cousins than the ones below, the [games like Wordle](/games/games-like-wordle/) page is the longer list.

## What belongs on this list?

A daily, for this page, means the site itself says a new puzzle shows up on a cadence, or it labels a mode "daily." A trivia library that hosts thousands of quizzes is listed separately, because "you could play it every day" is not the same product as "everyone shares today's puzzle." A geography game with no daily claim is listed as a neighbor, not as a streak game.

Account and price lines are only what each site's HTML stated on 15 November 2026. A page can still behave differently after JavaScript loads. JetPunk and Immaculate Grid blocked the earlier check, and Redactle did not show a description we could quote, so they are left out rather than described from memory. Those three were not reopened for this access pass.

## Which dailies reward the rarest answer?

**Rarest Answer, on this site.** A daily round deals up to seven shared prompts. With prompts in all eight categories, the day uses seven and one category sits out on a rotation. The least-viewed correct answer scores highest, on six tiers from 5 to 100 points, with one Spotlight double per round. No account. Stats stay in this browser. Detail lives in the [rules](/guides/how-to-play-rarest-answer/). [Unlimited play](/unlimited/) is there when you want reps beyond the daily. The technique is [finding rare answers](/guides/how-to-find-rare-answers/).

**Krillion, at [krillion.io](https://krillion.io/).** The homepage menu includes Daily dive, Unlimited, Puffer Party, Archive, and Themed packs. The [FAQ](https://krillion.io/faq) says answer sets are built by aggregating sources the question needs, then merging and cleaning them. It says some prompts are scored mechanically against a familiarity database, and others use a mix of model help and a manual pass. It also says the top "Krillion" pick is not chosen on rarity alone once answers get very deep: fun matters, because a legal answer nobody would ever type is a bad gem. Those are Krillion's words, paraphrased. This site does not use Krillion's tier names, and a fan site's point table is not repeated here. There is also a separate Roblox experience with Krillion in the title. That is not the browser FAQ above. Our [Krillion page](/games/krillion/) and the [games like Krillion](/games/games-like-krillion/) list keep those straight. The Roblox party with a similar slogan is [Rarest Answer Party](/guides/rarest-answer-party-roblox/), another product again.

If your actual goal is "name a thing, score more when it is obscure," stop at those two browser games and then practice. The rest of this page will not train that reflex. It will train spelling, semantic distance, or maps.

## Which Wordle-likes opened, and what do they say they are?

**[Wordle](https://www.nytimes.com/games/wordle/index.html)** (The New York Times). The page says: guess the hidden word in 6 tries, with a new puzzle each day.

**[Connections](https://www.nytimes.com/games/connections)** (The New York Times). Group words that share a common thread. A new puzzle is available each day.

**[Strands](https://www.nytimes.com/games/strands)** (The New York Times). The page's line is "Find hidden words and uncover the day's theme."

**[Spelling Bee](https://www.nytimes.com/puzzles/spelling-bee)** (The New York Times). The description we read was "How many words can you make with 7 letters?" A different path under `/games/spelling-bee` returned 404, so the puzzles path is the one to use.

**[Quordle](https://www.merriam-webster.com/games/quordle/)** (Merriam-Webster). The page says you solve four words at once, with 9 guesses to solve all four, and a new Quordle each day. The New York Times path `/games/quordle` returned 404 on this check, so this list points at Merriam-Webster.

**[Nerdle](https://nerdlegame.com/).** Titled "the daily numbers game." The description says guess the solution in 6 tries, and names classic, mini, speed, and pro.

**[Waffle](https://wafflegame.net/).** Titled "Waffle - daily word game," described as the waffle-shaped daily word game. The page does not, in that description, spell out the swap rules. Play the page rather than a recap.

**[Squaredle](https://squaredle.app/).** A daily word-building game: swipe letters in the grid.

**[Semantle](https://semantle.com/).** A daily word game where you hunt a hidden word through semantic clues, not letter coloring.

**[Contexto](https://contexto.me/).** Guess a secret word. Each guess is told how close it is, and the page credits an AI for that closeness.

**[Absurdle](https://qntm.org/absurdle)** by qntm, dated 2022-01-10 on the page. It feels like Wordle, but it does not commit to one secret word. It keeps every secret still consistent with your clues. That is a different contract from a shared daily answer, which is why it is listed separately.

None of these score a "rare" answer. They score a match to a hidden target. Wordle practice will not move a category tier, and [how to find a rare answer](/guides/how-to-find-rare-answers/) will not tell you tomorrow's word. Read what the puzzle actually asked.

## Which geography and picture dailies checked out?

**[Worldle](https://worldle.teuteuf.fr/).** A daily geography game: guess the country from its shape, get clues on each attempt, play the daily or an archive.

**[Globle](https://globle-game.com/).** The page says you use geography to figure out a mystery country in as few guesses as possible. A second site, globle.org, answered on 14 November 2026, titled "Globle Game - Guess the Country," and described itself as inspired by Wordle. It was not requested again on 15 November. Two domains, two pages. This list recommends the globle-game.com URL it quoted, and it does not claim the other domain is an alias.

**[Flagle](https://www.flagle.io/).** The page calls it a free daily flag game: guess the country from its flag in 6 tries, then bonus rounds for shape, capital, borders, and more. A teuteuf subdomain for Flagle did not complete a TLS handshake on this check, so the flagle.io URL is the one listed.

**[Travle](https://travle.earth/).** The description says it is a daily game about getting between countries in as few guesses as possible.

**[Tradle](https://oec.world/en/games/tradle)** (Observatory of Economic Complexity). Guess the place from what it exports: countries, or a state, province, or region. A shorter `/en/tradle/` path redirected; the games path is the one that returned the description.

**[Framed](https://framed.wtf/).** Guess the movie from 6 frames. The page says to come back each day, or use the archive for days you missed.

**[TimeGuessr](https://timeguessr.com/).** The description says it tests geography and history: guess where and when historic photos were taken. The page itself offers a Daily mode ("Play the daily challenge") alongside a random game and multiplayer. It is a daily if you choose that mode. It is not only a daily.

**[GeoGuessr](https://www.geoguessr.com/)** is a neighbor, not a daily here. Its homepage says it takes you around the world and asks you to recognize your surroundings. That text does not call it a once-a-day shared puzzle. It is a street view to interpret, not a flag with six tries.

## Where do you go for a pile of trivia that is not one shared puzzle?

**[Sporcle](https://www.sporcle.com/)** is a library of trivia quizzes on every topic, according to its title. A library is not one shared daily unless the quiz you opened says so.

**[Puzzmo](https://puzzmo.com/)** calls itself a home for thoughtful daily puzzles. It is a hub, not one ruleset. Open it for an editor's set of puzzles rather than another Wordle clone.

Category drills still help the rarest-answer game more than another word grid does. [Screen trivia](/trivia/screen/) and [animal trivia](/trivia/animals/) are two of the eight categories, and they practice naming a legal member instead of naming a hidden word.

[Stranger Things characters](/guides/stranger-things-characters-trivia/) are one worked example. The scored list is live at [the 129 scored names](/answers/stranger-things-characters/), and every other prompt is on [rarest answers](/rarest-answers/).

Pick one and return tomorrow. Wordle-likes train Wordle-likes, map games train maps, and a rarest-answer daily trains you to skip the highest-viewed legal name. They are all worth playing. They are not the same practice.
