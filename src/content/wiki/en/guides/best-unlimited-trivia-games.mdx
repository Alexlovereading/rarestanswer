The best unlimited trivia games are the ones that still exist after you finish today's puzzle. A shared daily is a good habit and a bad practice partner, because you only get it once. This list is the second kind of product: a library, an archive, a random mode, or a mode the site itself calls unlimited. Every URL was opened on 14 November 2026. A page that blocked the check is not filled in from memory.

Dailies that are only dailies, including the Wordle-likes, are already on [best daily trivia games](/guides/best-daily-trivia-games/). This page does not repeat them unless the same site also offers another way to play.

## What can you replay on this site?

[Unlimited mode](/unlimited/) deals a new set each time you start it. The tiers stay Common through Legendary, at 5, 10, 20, 40, 70, and 100 points. The clock stays 25 seconds unless you turn it off. Spotlight still doubles one scored prompt. The round does not move the daily streak, and it does not rewrite today's seven. Category pages do the same inside one topic. [Geography](/trivia/geography/) and [animals](/trivia/animals/) are two of the eight. If a category has no published prompts, the screen says so and leaves Play disabled.

That is the rarest-answer form of unlimited. The [rules page](/guides/how-to-play-rarest-answer/) is the table. The daily itself remains the [homepage](/), once per local date.

## What did Krillion actually publish about playing past the daily?

The menu on [krillion.io](https://krillion.io/) includes Unlimited. Opening [krillion.io/unlimited/classic](https://krillion.io/unlimited/classic) showed the daily-dive shell and the line "one in a krillion." It did not show a rules sheet, a price, or a statement that the URL is the free practice dive. The [privacy policy](https://krillion.io/privacy) describes that practice dive separately: answers are stored with a random id so scoring can be tuned. Those two facts stay unmerged.

The [packs page](https://krillion.io/packs) is clearer about extra play. It says themed packs are "Free for now · BETA," warns that answers and scoring may have issues, and lists four packs you can open: Words and Language (30 chapters), Movies (30 pictures), Sports (30 games), and Geography (30 expeditions). Music is marked coming soon. Archive is described as "Every aired daily, resurfaceable." That is a way to play a past dive again. It is not a promise about Unlimited. The [Krillion page](/games/krillion/) keeps the rest of their rules.

## Which quiz libraries opened, and what did they call themselves?

**[Sporcle](https://www.sporcle.com/).** The title is "Play Fun Trivia Quizzes on Every Topic." The homepage text includes "Unlimited quizzes and puzzle games" and "Let your brain play every day," next to a Daily Dose and featured quizzes. The [about page](https://www.sporcle.com/about/) says every day more than a million quizzes are played online, and, further down, that more than one million community-created quizzes are online, covering topics "from NFL trivia and the periodic table to world capitals." The [games page](https://www.sporcle.com/games/) calls itself "Your Daily Place to Play" and lists separate puzzle games, including Acrostic and WorldPlay, with archive links. A library plus a daily dose is unlimited in the sense used here. It is not a rarest-answer score.

**[FunTrivia](https://www.funtrivia.com/).** The title calls it the world's largest trivia site. The same homepage did not agree with itself, or with its quizzes index, about the size. The hero line read "2.5 Million Questions | 160,000 quizzes | 14,000 Topics" and "Updated Daily, Since 1995." A later heading on that page said "168,616 Trivia Quizzes." The quizzes index title said "155,501 Quizzes," and its meta description said "2 million trivia questions, 155,000 trivia quizzes." This guide does not pick a winner among those counts. What is stable on the page is the offer: topic quizzes you can start, plus a section named "Daily and Hourly Trivia Games," plus an "Educational Quizzes" line aimed at parents, teachers, and students. You do not have to wait for one shared puzzle.

**[TriviaPlaza](https://www.triviaplaza.com/).** The title is "Play online trivia quizzes and get graded." The page lists categories including Pop Music, Movie, Geography, Science, Computer, Literature, Classical Music, History, Television, Sports, Languages, and General Knowledge, and it shows recent film quizzes through 2025. No rarest-answer rule was on that page.

**[Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/quizzes).** The quizzes index is a magazine feed of staff quizzes, with dates, including geography and movie quizzes. It is not one shared daily and not a type-the-rarest-member game. Play the quiz that is actually on the page.

JetPunk, Britannica, Braingle, and PurposeGames did not return a readable quiz page. Quizizz redirected to wayground.com and the body was not readable text. They are omitted.

## Which geography games let you start again?

**[Seterra](https://www.seterra.com/).** The visible page says "Master Geography with +400 Map Quizzes" and "Play now Free!" The player URL [online.seterra.com/en](https://online.seterra.com/en) redirected to a GeoGuessr-hosted Seterra page that says you get over 400 customizable quizzes on countries, capitals, flags, oceans, lakes, and more. That is a pile of map quizzes, not a midnight lock. A correct pin is the whole score. It will not train you to skip the famous capital for a rarer legal one. Tips for that different skill are on the [geography guide](/guides/rarest-answer-game-tips-geography/).

**[World Geography Games](https://www.world-geography-games.com/).** The page says it offers entertaining, educational quiz games and calls them free teaching resources for geography class. Again, the goal is the answer they asked for.

**[GeoGuessr](https://www.geoguessr.com/).** The homepage says it takes you on a journey around the world and challenges you to recognize your surroundings. That text does not describe a single shared daily puzzle. It is a street view to read, and you can treat it as unlimited only in that weak sense: the homepage is not a "come back tomorrow" gate. It is a poor drill for naming a rare member of a category.

**[TimeGuessr](https://timeguessr.com/).** The page offers three starts: "Play a random game," "Multiplayer," and "Play the daily challenge." Random play is why it is on this list. The description says it tests where and when historic photos were taken.

**[Worldle](https://worldle.teuteuf.fr/).** The description says it is a daily geography game and that you can play daily or explore the archive. The archive is the unlimited part. The daily is still a daily.

## What should you use each one for?

Use this site's unlimited mode to practice refusing the first legal name. Use Sporcle, FunTrivia, TriviaPlaza, or Mental Floss when you want a quiz someone else already wrote. Use Seterra, World Geography Games, GeoGuessr, TimeGuessr, or the Worldle archive when the skill you want is a map. Use Krillion's archive, and its beta packs, when you want more of their dive, and do not invent rules for the Unlimited URL they have not written down.

None of the libraries will move a tier on tomorrow's rarest-answer prompt by themselves. They add names you might legally type. The rank still happens inside the prompt, after you have refused the famous one.
