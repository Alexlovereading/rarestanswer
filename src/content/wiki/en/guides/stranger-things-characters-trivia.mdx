Stranger Things characters make a sharp rare-answer prompt. Ask a room to name one and you will hear Eleven, Mike, Hopper, Dustin, Lucas, Will, and Steve. Those answers are right, and on the published list the famous ones sit in Common. The scored prompt is [Stranger Things characters](/answers/stranger-things-characters/): 129 names, from English Wikipedia's character list plus Wikipedia pageviews (2026-10). There is no Legendary row in that file. Epic is the top bucket, and many of those rows are names counted at a single pageview. The index of every prompt is [rarest answers](/rarest-answers/).

Every name below is from the [list of Stranger Things characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Stranger_Things_characters) on English Wikipedia, as it read on 14 November 2026. The Duffer Brothers' show is set in the 1980s in Hawkins, Indiana, around Will Byers going missing. That page dates the story from 1983 to 1987, with Netflix premieres on 15 July 2016, 27 October 2017, and 4 July 2019, a fourth season in May and July 2022, and a fifth, final season in three parts between November and December 2025. The list also covers the animated spin-off *Stranger Things: Tales from '85*. A prompt that means only the live-action series might not want a spin-off-only name. Those names are marked below.

The habit is the same one in [finding rare answers](/guides/how-to-find-rare-answers/): ban the poster, then pick someone whose job you can say in one sentence.

## Which Stranger Things characters does everyone name first?

Wikipedia's main-cast table is the poster. Joyce Byers (Winona Ryder), Jim Hopper (David Harbour), Mike Wheeler (Finn Wolfhard), Eleven / Jane Hopper (Millie Bobby Brown), Dustin Henderson (Gaten Matarazzo), Lucas Sinclair (Caleb McLaughlin), Nancy Wheeler (Natalia Dyer), and Jonathan Byers (Charlie Heaton) are main cast from season 1. Will Byers (Noah Schnapp) and Steve Harrington (Joe Keery) are also starring in season 1 and main from season 2. Karen Wheeler and Dr. Martin Brenner are main cast too. Typing any of them is the common move: correct, and crowded.

A second group is famous even with a shorter credit. Do not treat these as sneaky picks. Max Mayfield, Robin Buckley, Erica Sinclair, and Billy Hargrove are later main cast. Eddie Munson (Joseph Quinn) is only also starring in season 4, and people still volunteer him: Hellfire Club, blamed for Chrissy Cunningham's murder, killed by demobats. A short credit is not obscurity. Vecna / Henry Creel (Jamie Campbell Bower) leads season 4 and is main cast in season 5. Barb Holland (Shannon Purser) dies early and stays famous. Bob Newby (Sean Astin) dates Joyce and is killed by Demodogs in season 2. Argyle (Eduardo Franco) is the Surfer Boy Pizza driver in season 4. "The season 4 guy" is still the common half of memory. [Strategy](/guides/rarest-answer-strategy/) here is to leave the memorable face and keep the job.

## Who sits just off the poster?

These people matter in at least one season and are easier to blank on than Eddie or Steve. They are a middle band of memory. The published pageview rank is a different list. Murray Bauman and Kali Prasad are Familiar. Argyle and Holly Wheeler are Uncommon. Scott Clarke is Rare. Benny Hammond and Agent Stinson are Epic. Florence and Yuri Ismaylov are Common, because those stored view counts are high. Read the answer page before you treat a forgettable job as a high score.

Murray Bauman (Brett Gelman) is the investigator brought in around Barbara's disappearance, a former Chicago Sun-Times journalist. He speaks Russian, goes to Kamchatka with Joyce, and in season 5 smuggles supplies as "Austin." Kali Prasad, or Eight (Linnea Berthelsen), is another lab subject, with illusion powers. Eleven finds her in Chicago in season 2, with Axel, Dottie, Funshine, and Mick. In season 5, Dr. Kay's group has her. Eleven and Hopper get her out, she is fatally wounded, and Mike later theorizes that a last illusion let Eleven slip the military. "Eight" is a better shape than "Eleven," and it is obvious to anyone who watched that episode.

Holly Wheeler is a co-star (Anniston Price and Tinsley Price) until Nell Fisher plays her as season 5 main cast. Vecna approaches her as "Mr. Whatsit," from *A Wrinkle in Time*. She was an easy miss before the final season and may not be after it. Dr. Kay (Linda Hamilton) leads that season's military hunt. The list says she holds Kali and uses Kali's blood on pregnant women to restart Brenner's program. Suzie Bingham (Gabriella Pizzolo) is Dustin's camp girlfriend, found in Salt Lake City in season 4. The list calls her a hacker when it mentions her sister Eden. Vickie Dunne (Amybeth McNulty) is Robin's girlfriend by season 5.

## Which names are actually easy to overlook?

This is the useful band. Each person is on the Wikipedia list, with a job you can defend, and none of them leads a season.

Scott Clarke (Randy Havens) is the boys' science teacher. In season 5, Erica brings him in after Dustin is stuck in the Upside Down. He learns it is real and helps plan the fight against Vecna. Viewers often say "Mr. Clarke." The list's name is Scott Clarke.

Florence, Flo (Susan Shalhoub Larkin), is the secretary at the Hawkins police station. The list notes that she worries about Hopper's health. That is a whole character and almost nobody's first guess.

Benny Hammond (Chris Sullivan) owns Benny's Burgers, shelters Eleven after the lab, and is killed by lab staff. Connie Frazier (Catherine Dyer) is Brenner's enforcer, and Eleven kills her at the end of season 1. A lab surname is a rare-answer shape. Brenner himself is main cast.

Terry Ives (Aimee Mullins) is Eleven's biological mother. Becky Ives (Amy Seimetz) is Terry's sister, who the list says looks after her. Wikipedia flags one sentence about how Terry was left unresponsive as failed verification, so that cause is omitted here. The family tie is the sourced fact. Say the name, not "Eleven's mom," if the game wants a name.

Dr. Alexei (Alec Utgoff) is a season 3 Russian scientist on the gate project. Hopper and Joyce take him for what he knows. He likes the mall food and the cartoons. Grigori (Andrey Ivchenko), the enforcer Hopper kills at the end of that season, kills Alexei at the fair. People remember "the Russian scientist." The points are on "Alexei."

Dmitri Antonov, Enzo (Tom Wlaschiha), is the Kamchatka guard Hopper bribes. He is jailed for treason beside Hopper and gets out with Joyce and Murray. Yuri Ismaylov (Nikola Đuričko) is the pilot and smuggler they are sent to meet in Alaska. He betrays them, and they use him to enter the prison. Not "a Russian." The specific person.

Wayne Munson (Joel Stoffer) is Eddie's uncle. He believes Eddie is innocent, and Dustin tells him Eddie died a hero. Agent Stinson (Paris Benjamin) works for Sam Owens and helps Hopper, Joyce, and Murray get home. Owens is too central to count as overlooked. Stinson is the name beside that job.

Shorter still: Mayor Larry Kline (Cary Elwes), blackmailed into helping the season 3 Russians; Doris Driscoll (Peggy Miley), interviewed by Nancy and Jonathan and later possessed; Miss Harris (Hope Hynes Love), Holly's season 5 teacher; Derek Turnbow (Jake Connelly), a bully in Holly's class who is also taken. Each one is in the show, and none of them is Eleven.

The same article's spin-off section adds Nikki Baxter (Odessa A'zion), a gadget-building kid in *Tales from '85*, and her mother Anna Baxter (Janeane Garofalo), who subs for the science teacher. Rare beside the live-action leads, and possibly out of bounds if the prompt means only the original series.

## How should you use this in a rarest-answer round?

Do not memorize this page as points. The points are on the [answer page](/answers/stranger-things-characters/). A six-person party will still favor whoever the other five did not type, and that is not the same as the pageview tier. The browser rules are on [how to play](/guides/how-to-play-rarest-answer/).

For practice, ban the poster name you were about to type. Pick a job: teacher, secretary, diner owner, lab enforcer, uncle, pilot, mayor. Attach a name from this page only if you can spell it. Skip the spin-off unless the prompt invited it. Then check Wikipedia, not a fan ranking. Eddie's short credit is the warning: billing is not fame. The [daily trivia roundup](/guides/best-daily-trivia-games/) includes puzzles that want one hidden title. Played as a category, this show wants a real person from the uncrowded end of the cast, the same skill as naming the Tapanuli orangutan instead of "a gorilla."
