Stranger Things characters are a prompt on this site. The question is "Name a Stranger Things character." The published file has 122 accepted names. Twelve of them are ranked by a stored pageview total. The other 110 are accepted and unranked. The prompt sits in [screen trivia](/trivia/screen/), and the index of every prompt is [rarest answers](/rarest-answers/). Open the full table at [Stranger Things characters](/answers/stranger-things-characters/).

The source line is the English Wikipedia list of Stranger Things characters, read from cast tables, character headings, and recurring or guest credits, plus Wikipedia pageviews (2026-10). It says a name with no verifiable article, or only a redirect into that list, is unranked. Where a rank exists, it is that stored total compared only with the other ranked names. It is not a count of what players type, and it is not a billing order.

Checked against the published file on 17 November 2026, the 12 ranked names split into 3 Common, 2 Familiar, 2 Uncommon, 2 Rare, 2 Epic, and 1 Legendary.

## Which of the 12 ranked names are the poster characters?

Only a name that keeps a character article and a pageview total is ranked. Common, 5 points, is the high-view end of those 12: Vecna, Eleven, and Will Byers. Familiar, 10 points: Steve Harrington and Nancy Wheeler. Uncommon, 20 points: Jim Hopper and Mike Wheeler. Rare, 40 points: Max Mayfield and Joyce Byers. Epic, 70 points: Robin Buckley and Dustin Henderson. Legendary, 100 points: Ted Wheeler, stored popularity 31,966, wiki title Ted Wheeler (Stranger Things).

Vecna is the most-viewed stored row, Common, at popularity 1,611,977, wiki title Vecna (Stranger Things). A smaller stored total is a lower pageview rank inside these 12 names. It is not a separate measure of how recognizable the character is.

## Which accepted names have no pageview rank?

One hundred ten accepted names are unranked. They are not stored at one pageview, and they are not an Epic row. Eleven of them are unranked because the only measurement was a redirect into the character list: Scott Clarke, Derek Turnbow, Holly Wheeler, Lonnie Byers, Murray Bauman, Erica Sinclair, Bob Newby, Lucas Sinclair, Martin Brenner, Billy Hargrove, and Eddie Munson. The file does not keep a wiki title or a pageview total for those rows. Martin Brenner's stored aliases include Dr. Brenner and Papa, so those spellings are accepted and unranked too.

Eddie Munson is in that redirect group. He is not a ranked Common, Uncommon, or Legendary name. The answer page lists him as Uncommon (unranked).

The same unranked label covers names with no verifiable article. Jonathan Byers, Karen Wheeler, and Barbara Holland are in that set. Barbara Holland's stored alias is Barb. So are Argyle, Yuri Ismaylov, Troy Walsh, Nikki Baxter, Kali Prasad, the Mind Flayer, Connie Frazier, Suzie Bingham, Florence, Jason Carver, Benny Hammond, Agent Stinson, Wayne Munson, Dr. Alexei, and Dmitri Antonov. Troy is a stored alias of Troy Walsh, and Alexei is a stored alias of Dr. Alexei.

An unranked name is still a legal answer. It has no pageview rank, and it is not the rarest name on the list. The answer page lists it as Uncommon (unranked). A hit scores 20 points because that is the missing-measurement rule, the same points as a measured Uncommon row. The label means the measurement is absent. It does not mean an article was counted and landed on tier 3.
