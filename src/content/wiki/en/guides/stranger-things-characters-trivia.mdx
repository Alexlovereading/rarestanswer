Stranger Things characters are a scored prompt on this site. The question is "Name a Stranger Things character." The published file has 129 answers, in the screen category, and the index of every prompt is [rarest answers](/rarest-answers/). Open the full table at [Stranger Things characters](/answers/stranger-things-characters/).

The source line is English Wikipedia's list of Stranger Things characters, plus Wikipedia pageviews (2026-10). It also says a name with no article counts as 1. The rank is that view total inside this list only. It is not a count of what players type, and it is not a billing order.

Checked against the published file on 15 November 2026, the tiers are 26 Common, 26 Familiar, 25 Uncommon, 7 Rare, and 45 Epic. There is no Legendary row. All 45 Epic rows are stored at popularity 1 and share rarity 0.828. Epic is the top bucket in this file because those one-view names sit at the least-viewed end of this particular list.

## Which stored ranks are the poster names?

Common, 5 points, is the high-view end. The file stores Eleven, Mike Wheeler, Jim Hopper, Dustin Henderson, Will Byers, Steve Harrington, Nancy Wheeler, Joyce Byers, Max Mayfield, Robin Buckley, Vecna, Eddie Munson, and Billy Hargrove as Common. Lucas Sinclair is Familiar, 10 points, not Common. Jonathan Byers, Karen Wheeler, and Barbara Holland are Uncommon, 20 points. Barbara Holland's stored alias is Barb.

Eddie Munson is Common, with stored popularity 104332, and the JSON has no wiki title on that row. A short season-4 credit does not make the stored tier Epic. Read the answer page before you treat a famous later character as a high score.

## Which rows link a different article?

Some stored ranks follow an article that is not the character. The game still scores the stored tier. Do not use that tier as a measure of the character.

- Florence links to Florence and is Common.
- Connie Frazier links to United States Department of Energy and is Common.
- Suzie Bingham links to Mormons and is Common.
- Mind Flayer links to Illithid and is Common.

- Jason Carver links to Satanic panic and is Common.
- Kali Prasad links to Illusion and is Familiar.
- Nikki Baxter links to Stranger Things: Tales from '85 and is Common.

Argyle is Uncommon and the stored wiki title is Argyle. That title alone does not show it is a character article, so this page does not treat the rank as the character's own views. Murray Bauman is Familiar and has no wiki title. Holly Wheeler is Uncommon and has no wiki title. Scott Clarke is Rare, popularity 301, with no wiki title.

## Which names are stored at one pageview?

The source line's "no article counts as 1" matches the 45 Epic rows at popularity 1. Benny Hammond, Agent Stinson, Wayne Munson, Dr. Alexei, and Dmitri Antonov are in that group, and none of them has a wiki title in the file. A forgettable job can land there. It can also land in Common when the link is a busy unrelated article, which is what the Yuri and Connie rows do.

Fifty-five answers have no wiki title. Ten of those have popularity above 1, including Eddie Munson, Billy Hargrove, Lucas Sinclair, and Murray Bauman. A blank wiki field is not a promise of one view.

The same source line includes the animated spin-off. A spin-off-only name can still be accepted. Nikki Baxter is the example above: the linked article is the spin-off page, and the stored tier is Common.

## How should you use this in a round?

Do not memorize this page as points. The points are the stored pageview tiers on the [answer page](/answers/stranger-things-characters/). The browser rules, including the 25-second clock and Spotlight, are on [how to play](/guides/how-to-play-rarest-answer/). A name you can spell that is actually on the list beats a name you only remember as "the Russian" or "the teacher." If the answer page shows a link to a different article, the tier is that article's views.
