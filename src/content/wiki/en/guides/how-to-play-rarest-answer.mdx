The rarest answer game on this site is a trivia round with the usual goal flipped. You still have to be right. Being right in the way everyone else is right barely moves the score. The rarest answer wins the prompt, and the player who does that across the round wins the round.

You can start a daily round from the [homepage](/). This page is the rule card: what a round contains, what the six tiers pay, what Spotlight does, and how unlimited play differs. How to think of the rare name is the [finding guide](/guides/how-to-find-rare-answers/). When to spend the boost is [strategy](/guides/rarest-answer-strategy/).

## What kind of game is a rarest answer game?

Each turn shows one prompt, written as an instruction such as "Name a species of great ape." You type a single answer. The game accepts the name if it is on that prompt's list, including alternate spellings stored with the answer. Capitalization, accents, punctuation, and a leading word such as "the" should not turn a good answer into a miss. A one-character typo on a longer name can still match. A different name cannot.

If the answer is accepted, it already has a tier inside that prompt. The tier is not a grade of how "clever" the sentence sounded. It is a bucket on a rarity rank from 1 to 6, where 6 is the rarest. Two different names can share a bucket. Points come from the bucket, then from Spotlight if you spent it on that prompt.

If the answer is not accepted, the prompt does not pay. Obscurity is not a wildcard. The [Roblox party game with a similar name](/guides/rarest-answer-party-roblox/) uses the same slogan, rarer answers score more, but it is a different product with its own room of players. The rules on this page are the browser game.

## How does the daily round work?

A daily round is seven prompts. Everyone gets the same seven. That is what makes the score comparable: you are not hunting a private question, you are answering the set the rest of the day's players saw.

The set resets at local midnight. A countdown on the daily screen tells you how long the current set has left. Play the round once for the day's official result. Your streak and your stats stay in the browser, on this device, rather than on an account. Clearing site data clears them. A different browser does not inherit them.

You can turn the timer off if you want the same prompts without the clock. Leaving it on is the normal daily. Either way, submit before you leave the prompt. An empty box cannot be tiered.

After the seventh prompt, the end screen shows the total, a breakdown by tier, and a way to share. Sharing can use the device share sheet or the clipboard, as a text grid, and you can download a share image. "Play another" leaves the daily and continues elsewhere. It does not rewrite the day's seven.

The round is playable from the keyboard. Timer and score updates are announced for screen readers. If your system asks for reduced motion, the tier animation respects that. None of that changes the points.

## What do the six tiers mean?

These names belong to this game. They are not the tier names used by Krillion or by any Roblox experience.

| Tier | Name | Points | How to read it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Common | 5 | The answer the room already had |
| 2 | Familiar | 10 | A standard textbook or poster answer |
| 3 | Uncommon | 20 | Correct, and off the first reply |
| 4 | Rare | 40 | A specific member most players skip |
| 5 | Epic | 70 | Far down the list, still clearly legal |
| 6 | Legendary | 100 | The rarest bucket on that prompt |

A perfect rare round is seven Legendary answers: 700 points before any boost. A round of seven common-but-correct answers is 35. Both rounds are "all right." The gap is the game. That is also why a single blank hurts more than it does in a puzzle that only counts solves. Zero on a prompt is a missed chance at up to 100, not a small penalty.

The sample great-ape prompt shows tiers that are not evenly filled. Human is Common. Chimpanzee and Western gorilla are Familiar. Bonobo and Bornean orangutan are Uncommon. Sumatran orangutan is Rare. Eastern gorilla is Epic. Tapanuli orangutan is Legendary. You do not need this table memorized for that one question. You need the habit: the unspecialized name sits on the left, the place-specific species sits on the right. Walkthrough of that list: [finding rare answers](/guides/how-to-find-rare-answers/).

## What is the Spotlight boost?

Spotlight is a 2x boost you can use once per round. It doubles the points of one prompt. The arithmetic is small and worth doing before you spend it.

- Common doubled is 10.
- Familiar doubled is 20.
- Uncommon doubled is 40.
- Rare doubled is 80.
- Epic doubled is 140.
- Legendary doubled is 200.

The boost does not turn a rejected answer into a score, and it does not change which tier the name was. It multiplies the tier you actually hit. Spending it on a prompt where you are about to type the obvious name converts 5 into 10. Spending it on a prompt where you have a specific, defensible, uncommon name converts a large tier into a larger one. You only get one. The [strategy page](/guides/rarest-answer-strategy/) covers when to hold it.

Spotlight is the name of this boost. It is not a difficulty, a currency, or a second game mode.

## How is unlimited mode different from the daily?

The daily is one shared set, once, with a streak attached. [Unlimited mode](/unlimited/) is how you keep playing after that set, or instead of treating today's seven as the only reps you get. Scoring uses the same six tiers. Unlimited is the place to test a technique you do not want to gamble on a streak: going one step more specific, or holding Spotlight until the fifth prompt.

Unlimited is not a promise that you are replaying someone else's published answer key, and it is not the Roblox party. It is extra play on this site, outside the single daily round.

You can also play one category at a time. The categories are geography, animals, food, sports, screen, music, science, and history. An animals session stays on animals. That is the right room if you are drilling the great-ape habit without a sports prompt in the middle. Open a category from trivia, for example [animal trivia](/trivia/animals/) or [screen trivia](/trivia/screen/).

The optional timer control applies here too. Turn it off when you are learning the move. Turn it on when you want the daily's pressure without using up the daily.

## What do you see after you submit?

The reveal for a prompt shows the tier your answer hit, a meter for that tier, the top 10 rarest answers, the most common answers, and how many answers you could have named from the list. Read the common column before you admire the rare column. The common column is the list of things you should have refused to type. The rare column is a menu of shapes, not a list to memorize for a different prompt tomorrow.

"How many you could have named" is a humility check. A Legendary hit on a list of eight is a different skill from a Legendary hit on a list of eight hundred. The reveal tells you which one you just played.

Then the next prompt starts clean. Tiers do not carry a bonus forward except through the running total, and except through Spotlight if you have not used it yet. At the end, the tier breakdown is the honest recap: how many of the seven landed in each bucket. A high total made of one Legendary and six blanks is a different game from a high total made of seven Rare answers. Both can be worth learning from. Only one of them is repeatable.
