Checked in a browser on 16 November 2026. No round was started on any site, so this note does not quote a live prompt from inside a running game.   - Email: support@krillion.to
  - Site delivery line names Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA
- The legal notice does not mention krillion.io or krillion.org.
- Rules page: https://krillion.to/en/how-to-play (title “How to Play KRILLION: Rules and Scoring · KRILLION”).
- FAQ: https://krillion.to/en/faq
- Pack index: https://krillion.to/en/packs

Rules stated on the how-to page and repeated in the FAQ:

- A game is 7 questions. The question shows for 3 seconds, then the player has 25 seconds to type one recognized answer. It is not multiple choice.
- Six editor-assigned tiers, not measured shares of player answers: Plankton 10, Too Clever 15, Schooler 30, Rare 60, Deep Cut 85, Krillion 100. Maximum 700 points. Depth is 10 metres per point, so 700 points is 7,000 metres.
- The Daily Dive is the same for everyone and is derived from the date.
- Four free packs: pop culture, movies and TV, sport, and geography. Together they are 824 numbered games. Paid access opens four more packs and 422 further games (1,246 games).
- Free play is 3 games a day. The first game of the day does not need an account. A free account is what opens all 3. A footer line on the movies page and the pack pages read “1 free game, then 3 a day with an account”.
- Prices on the how-to page: a 15-day free trial for a first subscription, with an account, then €30.00 a year (stated as €2.50 a month); or €3.50 a month with no trial; or €29.90 once. The FAQ states the same prices and says there are no ads.
- English and French.
- Jelly is a separate multiplayer mode: 2 to 16 players, a nickname, no account, described as free and unlimited.
